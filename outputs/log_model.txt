STEP 2  |  Leakage check - why the full cycle is off limits
==============================================================================
corr(full discharge duration, capacity label) = 0.9975
-> 0.997 is not a model, it is the label rewritten. Full-cycle duration,
   total Ah and total Wh are therefore BANNED from the feature set.
-> Every feature below is computed from the first 600 s only.
   Shortest cycle in the data is 2108 s, so the
   window always exists and we never pad or extrapolate.

about 29%, and sure enough it holds to 15% and then collapses.
